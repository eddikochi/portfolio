About

I started in visual creation. Today, I organize digital complexity.

My trajectory started in design and evolved through layers — UX, CRM, data, CRO and digital governance. It wasn't a career change, it was an accumulation: each step, an adaptation to more complex problems.

Where I come from

Creative roots

The creative background is not a limitation to overcome — it's the repertoire that still sustains how I read hierarchy, clarity and language today.

How I evolved

A sequence of layers, not a career change

Design → UX/UI → CRM → Data → CRO → Digital Governance → Applied AI → Design Engineering

UX brought behavior and journey; CRM, performance; data, the foundation for decisions; CRO, measurement; governance, a response to operational bottlenecks. Complementary layers that converge into data-driven digital experience, optimization and governance.

What I do today

Digital experience, with real execution

I work across UX, CRM, data and governance — at the strategic and execution levels. My strength lies in connecting areas and organizing chaos, not just producing screens.

Where I'm heading

Design Engineering and autonomy

I've been developing technical skills toward Design Engineering: not leaving UX, CRO or governance behind, but combining them with greater technical autonomy. I treat the portfolio as a product — this site is part of that practice: handcrafted, documented, evolving.
