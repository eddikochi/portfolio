About

I started in visual design. Today I own the whole digital experience, from the screens to the operation behind them.

I added a new layer with each harder problem: UX, then CRM, data, CRO and digital governance. I never dropped what I had learned before — it all stacked up.

Where I come from

Roots in design

What I learned in design — hierarchy, clarity, language — is the base I still use to read any screen.

How I evolved

Layers that stacked up

Design → UX/UI → CRM → Data → CRO → Digital Governance → Applied AI

Each layer solved a limit of the previous one: UX gave me behavior and journey; data, a basis to decide; CRO, how to measure; governance, how to keep the operation from stalling. Today all of it works together day to day.

What I do today

Digital experience, with real execution

I work across UX, CRM, data and governance, in both strategy and execution. What I do best is connecting these areas and bringing order to a messy operation — screens are only one part of that.

Where I am headed

More technical autonomy, without dropping strategy

I have been expanding my technical execution — building, prototyping and shipping on my own — to shrink the distance between the idea and what goes live. I treat the portfolio as a product: this site is part of that practice, built and documented by hand.
